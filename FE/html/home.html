Down the Hellhole is a first person survival horror game currently in development.
Play as Christian as he awakens in a mysterious facility, with no place to go but foward he begins his search for a way out. However his goal changes quickly once he hears a familliar voice calling to him from behind a locked door, beckoning him to find it, a task easier said than done.
As Christitan and armed with your wits and a trusty hammer you must survive the otherwordly and deadly experiments that now lurk the abandoned halls of the facility as you make your way to your end goal.
Solve puzzles, fight to survive and unravel the mystery behind the facility and it's denizens
